Your words stay yours

Entries and saved reflections are stored in this browser's local storage, not our database. Anyone with access to this browser profile or an exported backup may be able to read them. Private/incognito browsing, clearing browser data, changing devices, or storage failures may erase them.

When you intentionally request an AI reflection, the relevant writing is sent through our server to Anthropic for processing. Our app does not intentionally log journal text. Third party processing and technical logging may still apply.

Optional activity analytics record a random participant ID, event type, challenge day, and timestamp. No journal content or mood is sent as analytics.

Deleting local data cannot retract writing previously sent to an AI provider. Export files are unencrypted: keep them secure.
